# Code of Conduct

Grassfield Notes is a calm app, and we want its community to be calm too: welcoming, patient and kind.

## Our commitment
Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space) should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their experience, background, identity, language or abilities.

## What we expect
- Be respectful and assume good intentions. Most misunderstandings are just misunderstandings.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a language that isn't their first.
- Respect people's time: stay on topic, and say so when you can no longer follow up on something you started.
- Credit other people's work.

## What we don't accept
- Insults, slurs, personal or political attacks, and demeaning jokes.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact, stalking and intimidation.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately. Use GitHub's private reporting tools, or the contact listed on the repository's profile, rather than a public issue. Every report is handled confidentially and as quickly as possible. Maintainers who are involved in a report step aside from handling it.

Possible responses, depending on severity: a private reminder, a public warning, a temporary ban from the project spaces, or a permanent ban.

## Scope
This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
